# Chinese civic and community recipe

## Visual thesis

Ceremonial, grounded, and contemporary. Express collective participation and Chinese cultural character without turning every page into a festival poster.

## Signature

- Use deep red, warm cream, muted gold, and dark ink as a disciplined palette.
- Combine stable symmetry with gentle circular or radial motifs that suggest gathering, community, and continuity.
- Use Chinese architectural, neighborhood, volunteer, or community-life imagery as a background or wide contextual field when appropriate.
- Keep the cover centered or optically centered, with a modest title and strong ceremonial calm.
- Use rectangles, bands, seals, circles, and line motifs in balance; do not let one shape family dominate every page.

## Typography and rhythm

Use authoritative Chinese typography with tighter line spacing than celebratory event posters. Keep titles compact and aligned to a consistent ceremonial axis. Use denser body layouts for schedules, activity plans, responsibilities, and outcomes. Preserve visible margins and avoid scattering text across the page.

## Useful silhouettes

- Centered cover over a quiet community background
- Theme statement + contextual image + participation structure
- Activity agenda + roles + venue/time information
- Community map or service stations + action list
- Three-stage participation process + responsibilities
- Outcomes, stories, and next community actions

## Remix controls

- More governmental: increase symmetry, reduce illustration, strengthen red bands and metadata.
- More community-centered: use warmer photography and human-scale stories.
- More cultural: introduce paper, seal, lattice, or landscape cues with restraint.
- More youthful: brighten accent red and use simpler contemporary shapes.

## Failure signals

Every module is circular; red and gold cover the entire page; the cover title is oversized or off-center; decorative symbols compete with activity information; Chinese character is reduced to generic festive ornament; photography feels pasted on rather than integrated.
